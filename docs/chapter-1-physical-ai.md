# Chapter 1: Introduction to Physical AI

## Understanding Physical AI

Physical AI is the integration of artificial intelligence with the physical world. It encompasses systems that can perceive, reason, and act in real-world environments. Unlike traditional AI that operates primarily in digital spaces, Physical AI systems interact directly with the physical environment through sensors and actuators.

## Core Components

Physical AI systems consist of three primary components:

1. **Sensors** - Collect data from the environment
2. **AI Models** - Process information and make decisions
3. **Actuators** - Execute actions in the physical world

## Applications

Physical AI has diverse applications, including:

- Autonomous vehicles
- Robotics
- Smart cities
- Healthcare systems
- Industrial automation

## Learning Objectives

After reading this chapter, you should be able to:

- Define Physical AI and its key characteristics
- Identify the core components of Physical AI systems
- Describe practical applications of Physical AI
- Understand the challenges in implementing Physical AI systems

## Key Terms

- **Physical AI**: AI systems that interact with the physical world
- **Sensors**: Devices that detect environmental inputs
- **Actuators**: Components that perform physical actions
