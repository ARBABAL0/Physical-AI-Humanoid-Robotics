# Introduction to Physical AI

## Welcome to Physical AI

Welcome to the comprehensive textbook on Physical AI. This book provides a thorough exploration of how artificial intelligence integrates with physical systems to create intelligent machines that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world.

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that interact directly with the physical environment. Unlike traditional AI that operates primarily in digital domains, Physical AI systems:

- **Perceive** the physical world through sensors
- **Process** information using AI algorithms
- **Act** upon the environment through actuators

## Book Structure

This textbook is organized to provide both theoretical understanding and practical applications:

1. **Foundations**: Core concepts and components
2. **Technologies**: Detailed exploration of sensors, AI models, and actuators
3. **Applications**: Real-world implementations and case studies
4. **Future Directions**: Emerging trends and research areas

This book is designed for:
